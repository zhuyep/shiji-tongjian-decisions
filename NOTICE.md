# Attribution and separate text licensing

The MIT license applies to original code, skill instructions and original analysis by this project. It does not replace the terms associated with quoted source texts.

The `skills/shiji-tongjian-decisions/references/sample-corpus/texts/` files contain selected Chinese Wikisource passages. Quotations and source contexts in `examples/evidence.json`, `examples/cases.json` and the worked example derive from those passages. Preserve their source attribution and the [text license notice](skills/shiji-tongjian-decisions/references/sample-corpus/LICENSE.md) when redistributing them.

The classical works are attributed to Sima Qian (《史记》) and Sima Guang and collaborators (《资治通鉴》). The electronic editions are supplied by Chinese Wikisource contributors. Ancient underlying works, modern punctuation, editorial changes and website licensing are distinct; this package retains the site's CC BY-SA 4.0 notice for the supplied electronic excerpts rather than claiming all third-party material as MIT.

Sources, revision IDs where available, retrieval dates, passage scopes and SHA-256 hashes are recorded in the [manifest](skills/shiji-tongjian-decisions/references/sample-corpus/manifest.json). Changes: selected passages; removed webpage footnote link markers; preserved textual/editorial brackets. No complete third-party repository or book is bundled.

Design references: [Chinese-History](https://github.com/YuanZHAO321/Chinese-History), [zizhitongjian-skill](https://github.com/sddzlsc/zizhitongjian-skill), and [shiji-kb](https://github.com/baojie/shiji-kb). The [design provenance](skills/shiji-tongjian-decisions/references/design-provenance.md) records pinned revisions and the specific ideas considered.
